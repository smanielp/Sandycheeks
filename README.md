# Sandycheeks — Powered Beach Wagon Venture

Organizational toolset and working repository for a new hardware venture: a
purpose-built, marine-grade **powered beach wagon** that assists the puller
over soft sand. A tracked variant is planned as a second model / halo product.

> **Status:** Concept / pre-validation. Nothing here is committed strategy yet —
> these are living documents meant to be argued with and revised.

## Working name

The venture has no chosen name yet. `Sandycheeks` is the repo codename.
Naming is a deliberate later decision (see `roadmap-and-next-steps.md`), made
after positioning is locked. Candidate directions are parked, not chosen.

## The one-line concept

> Pedal-assist for your beach wagon. A premium, corrosion-proof powered wagon
> that does the hauling so you can get your gear all the way to the water —
> not abandon it halfway up the dry sand.

## Founder's origin story

Hauling a wide-wheel beach wagon up soft sand took enormous effort. We couldn't
go far, and without one strong puller we'd have abandoned the wagon high on the
beach instead of getting it down to the water. The dry sand at the top of the
beach is the worst case — and the thing the product must be designed against.
