## Questions this document will answer

When filled in, this document will define:

1. **Brand name** (see `brand/naming.md` for candidate parking lot)

2. **Positioning statement**
   - For whom? (beachhead persona, then expansion)
   - What category? (how we want customers to mentally file us)
   - What's the key benefit?
   - Against whom? (the alternative the customer was using before)

3. **Target aesthetic / personality**
   - Who does this feel like? (reference brands, not competitors)
   - Candidates to evaluate against: Yeti (premium outdoor durability), Brompton
     (design-led, functional, slightly cult), Traeger (category-creating, enthusiast
     community), Hobie (coastal, serious, trusted by anglers)
   - What does it explicitly *not* feel like? (cheap electric cart, medical scooter,
     golf cart, kids' toy)

4. **Tone of voice**
   - How we write and speak about the product
   - What we claim vs. what we let the product prove
   - The Yeti model: show the product working in extreme conditions, let the
     customer conclude it's tough — don't just say "marine-grade"

5. **Tagline candidates**
   - "Pedal assist for your wagon" (functional, clear, concept-forward)
   - Others TBD

6. **Visual identity principles** (defer design execution to a designer;
   define the principles here)
   - Color palette direction
   - Typography character
   - Photography style
