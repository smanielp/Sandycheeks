## The real gap

Three things already exist; the combination does not:

1. **Big-wheel / balloon-tire beach carts** — solve soft sand, but are manual.
   (Mac Sports, Sea Striker, Rio, Gorilla Carts, WonderFold, many no-name.)
2. **Powered utility / garden wagons** — solve effort, but are built for lawns
   and gravel, not salt + soft sand. (Sun Joe and a wave of Alibaba-sourced
   electric carts.)
3. **Powered tracked carriers** — exist for construction (powered wheelbarrows)
   and some DIY beach builds, but nothing premium and purpose-built.

**The gap = a purpose-built, marine-grade, premium powered wagon for soft sand,
with the brand and design to match.** The opportunity is positioning and
engineering execution, not novel invention.

## Market sizing logic (top-down + bottom-up to reconcile)

- **[VALIDATE]** US beachgoing households / coastal second-home owners as TAM.
- **[VALIDATE]** Surf/beach angler population as SAM (the beachhead).
- Bottom-up SOM: reachable angler communities × realistic conversion ×
  price point. Build this from real community sizes, not hand-waving.
- Adjacent demand signals to check: sales velocity and review counts of existing
  beach wagons on Amazon; search volume for "beach cart soft sand,"
  "electric beach wagon," "motorized beach cart."

## Beachhead validation plan (surf anglers)

- Where they are: surf-fishing forums, regional Facebook groups, tournament
  rosters, coastal tackle shops, YouTube surf-fishing channels.
- What to learn: load weight, haul distance, frequency, current cart, biggest
  pain, willingness to pay for powered assist, and what would make them *not*
  buy (weight, battery anxiety, corrosion distrust, theft).
- Target: 20–30 structured conversations before committing to a prototype path.

## Willingness-to-pay probes

- Anchor against e-bikes ($1–3k) and premium coolers (Yeti, $300–1,300) — both
  prove this customer pays a premium for marine/outdoor durability and brand.
- Test price points $800 / $1,200 / $1,800 / $2,500 in discovery.

## Demand-validation tactics (cheap, before building much)

- Landing page + waitlist with a real price and a "reserve" button; measure
  conversion and email capture from targeted angler/beach-town traffic.
- A short demo video of an ugly working prototype hauling a loaded cart up dry
  sand effortlessly. This single artifact will do more than any spec sheet.
